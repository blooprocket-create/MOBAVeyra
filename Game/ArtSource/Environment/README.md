# Crucible environment kit

Veyra-authored source geometry for an overgrown highland ruin (ADR-040). `CrucibleKit.json` owns each family's parameters and seed and the kit's look (stone tints, moss, glyphs, foliage, wind); it contains no map coordinates and no gameplay collision. The generator is `Game/Scripts/GenerateEnvironmentMeshes.py` (Blender 5.2, background). No third-party mesh or texture content is incorporated: the kit's materials use the terrain's own generated textures (`Terrain/`).

Run `Game/Scripts/BuildEnvironmentArt.ps1 -Blender <executable>` from the repository root to regenerate the FBX files and source manifest and import them. Use `-ImportOnly` to import unchanged FBX. The manifest hashes what the meshes are made from, which is the whole kit except its `look` (`Game/Scripts/EnvironmentKit/inputs.py`; CI checks the committed manifest against the kit). A change to a family, the seed or the preview needs the full run. A change to the `look` needs only its materials rebuilt, with no mesh imported: `-Materials M_CrucibleGlyph` (names as listed below). The generator keeps every FBX whose content comes out unchanged, its bytes and its hash, and records each mesh's `contentSha256` (`Game/Scripts/fbx_content.py`). A run that changes no mesh therefore rewrites no FBX, though Blender stamps each export with its time. Pass `-- --only Cliff,Tree --preview` to the generator directly for a contact sheet in `Game/Saved/EnvironmentKit/kit_preview.png`.

Families: slate cliffs and river boulders (cut into facets, ledged and weathered), fluted pillars broken off, masonry blocks, steles with raised Flux glyphs on their own `Glyph` slot, trees, shrubs, ferns, reeds and grass. Material slots are named (`Rock`, `Ruin`, `Glyph`, `Bark`, `Leaves`, `Grass`) and the importer assigns materials by name. Foliage carries its tint in vertex colour and its height share in vertex alpha, for the wind.

Generator v4 replaces smooth canopy masses with layered clusters and folded edge leaves, bounded by each original mass ellipsoid. Tree/shrub controls are `leafClusters`, `clusterFlatten` and `edgeLeaves`; grass/reed controls are `bladeWidthRatio`, `leanRatio` and `bladeFold`. Regenerate with Blender 5.2 through the full build command above. Seed 48173 and placements are retained; the changed families are Tree, Shrub, Reeds and Grass. Check all six audit cameras on high/low, then validate motion, canopy obstruction and GPU cost before release. The extra silhouette geometry remains subject to each family's triangle budget.

The importer builds the kit's materials (`M_CrucibleRock`, `M_CrucibleRuin`, `M_CrucibleBark`, `M_CrucibleGlyph`, `M_CrucibleLeaves`, `M_CrucibleGrass`; they must compile, or it fails), imports each mesh fresh, gives stone Nanite, and checks dimensions, the base pivot and the absence of any collision; its report is `Game/Saved/EnvironmentKit/unreal-validation.json`. The glyphs' glow ignores the scene's exposure, so it reads the same under the Crucible's physical sun as anywhere: `glyphStrength` is in multiples of what the exposure maps to white (`Game/Scripts/KitMaterials/spec.py` checks the glyph values, in CI too). Placement is `VeyraDressingBuild` in VeyraWorldTools, from the style profile's `dressingProfile`.

Generated assets belong in `Game/Content/Veyra/World/Environment`. Committed binary assets need the repository's LFS lock before they are regenerated. Never edit FBX or UAssets as an independent authoring source.

Grass and reeds use Two Sided Foliage shading with their data-owned colour feeding both surface and transmission; solid leaf masses use Default Lit. See [Asset & VFX Pipeline section 10](../../../Docs/Production/VEYRA_ASSET_AND_VFX_PIPELINE.md#10-foliage). To reproduce the ground/foliage material pass, run `Game/Scripts/BuildTerrainArt.ps1`, then `Game/Scripts/BuildEnvironmentArt.ps1 -Materials M_CrucibleGrass,M_CrucibleLeaves`. This does not regenerate geometry or change the kit seed (48173). Review `Play_Crossing_Mid`, `Play_Jungle_A0`, `Play_DenseFog` and `Play_Mid_Middle` through `CaptureBattleground.ps1` on high and low profiles; animation, combat and packaged performance still need live validation.

For the cliff and shoreline material pass, rebuild terrain art and then `Game/Scripts/BuildEnvironmentArt.ps1 -Materials M_CrucibleRock,M_CrucibleRuin,M_CrucibleBark`. The shared projection helper and moss normal coverage follow [Asset & VFX Pipeline section 5.4](../../../Docs/Production/VEYRA_ASSET_AND_VFX_PIPELINE.md#54-uv-strategy). Water roughness/ripple strength and shore colour/normal strength remain in `Terrain/TerrainTextures.json`. Include `Play_Wall_00` and `Play_Wall_03` in the camera review. This pass changes surface response, not river shape, crossings, collision, landscape or dressing placement.

Moss colour and foliage detail use the complete XYZ projection to avoid side-projection streaks on upward-facing surfaces. Reproduce this correction with `Game/Scripts/BuildEnvironmentArt.ps1 -Materials M_CrucibleRock,M_CrucibleRuin,M_CrucibleBark,M_CrucibleLeaves,M_CrucibleGrass`, then capture the six cameras above on high and low profiles. No terrain texture or mesh regeneration is needed for this projection-only correction.

Moss cushion colour and height contrast are editable as `cushionContrast` and `cushionRelief` in `Terrain/TerrainTextures.json` (range 0–1). Run `Game/Scripts/BuildTerrainArt.ps1` to regenerate and import; existing environment materials reference the shared textures and need no rebuild. Review ground, caps and foliage on both profiles, following [Asset & VFX Pipeline section 8.3](../../../Docs/Production/VEYRA_ASSET_AND_VFX_PIPELINE.md#83-painterly-realistic-from-gameplay-distance).

Shoreline texture polish uses the Shore layer's existing `pebbleCover`, `roughness` and `normalStrength` controls. Rebuild terrain art and review `Play_Crossing_Mid` and `Play_Mid_Middle` on both profiles for sand/pebble separation, wet-edge glare and crossing readability. These controls change surface appearance only; the uniform bank footprint and decorative placement require a separate source-authoring pass.
