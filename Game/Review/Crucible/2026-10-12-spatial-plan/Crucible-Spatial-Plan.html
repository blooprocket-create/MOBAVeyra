Crucible redesign · design proposal for review

Crucible Spatial Plan

A whole-map plan to agree before any terrain is generated. It places the playable spaces first: lanes, walled keeps, camp clearings, jungle routes, the river and its Flux Wells. Whatever is left over becomes rock and forest. Team B's half is Team A's turned half a turn, so every route, camp and objective is equally fair to both sides.

Revisions from your notes

Room for more jungle camps

Two more camps per team, ringed on the map: one in the top-corner pocket by the outer ford, which the other team can contest from its outer jungle, and one in the bottom outer strip with its own lane mouth. Each team now has 8 camps: 4 inner and 4 outer.

The river just flows off the map

The walkable river now ends 700 past each outer ford at a rock lip, then drops through a gorge of falls to the map edge. Nobody can wade out of the map.

Space the middle turrets further apart

Mid Spires now stand 800, 3,000 and 5,200 from the inhibitor (gaps of 2,200, was 1,900). I read "middle turrets" as the mid lane Spires; if you meant the two base towers by the Prime Well, I'll spread those instead.

Spires shouldn't sit in the middle of the lane

Each Spire now stands 450 from the lane's centre line, on the shoulder beside the road, alternating sides along the lane. Waves walk past it on the road. Sides are measured from each owner's walking direction, so both teams' towers stay equivalent.

21 banks per team, up from 12. Nine are ribbons that hug the outside of a lane shoulder at lane mouths, mid doors and elbow insides, for flanks and escapes. Still none on a road or shoulder, and every lobe stays at least 599 from a structure, so towers are never hidden.

What the plan answers

The layout barely changed

Every space is new. Side lanes bend through rounded elbows, keeps are walled, the outer jungle is twice as deep, and the jungle is a network of clearings and routes instead of an open field with strips.

Walls consumed playable jungle

Routes and clearings are placed first and keep their full width. Interior masses cover 25% of the jungle, against 28% on main today, as solid forms at least 300 thick. Rims framing the outer routes against the boundary cliffs add 20%.

No Dense Fog in lanes

21 fog banks per team (48 lobes) sit at jungle mouths, river entrances, outer bends and along lane edges. None overlaps a lane road or shoulder.

Bases need walls and real entrances

Each keep has a stone perimeter with three 900-wide gates, one per lane. Inhibitors stand just inside the gates, equally far from the Prime Well. The well sits on a central plaza, with the fountain in the back corner.

One section can't fix the whole map

This is the whole map at once. Nothing gets art or detail until the composition is agreed.

Should we use Mesh Terrain?

The plan doesn't depend on it. Landscape plus cliff meshes can build every form here. Mesh Terrain remains a separate evaluation that would need its own ADR.

How the jungle is laid out

  • Three route widths, not uniform tubes. Rotation routes are 1,000 wide (crossroads to camps and the river). Lane approaches and back doors are 700. The wraparound outer routes are 900.
  • Clearings sized for fights. Camps have radius 800 (Skittermaw's pack 900). Crossroads, glades and river mouths are 800 to 900. Well arenas are 1,350 around a 700 island. The mid ford is 1,100.
  • Masses are what's left. Any leftover thinner than 300 or smaller than 25 m² becomes open ground, so no raised strips survive.
  • Readable lanes. Each camp has 3 to 5 entrances. Each team's lane legs get 3 inner and 3 outer mouths.
  • Top and bot feel different. The top inner jungle is a crossroads hub with a loop by the river. The bot side runs a low path along the lane between its two camps. The left outer strip loops around a central mass; the bottom one is a single long route of glades.
  • Four camps outside, four inside. Each outer strip holds two camps, so a second roamer has real farm without taking the inner jungle's.
  • Outer fords. Where the river leaves the map's corners, each team's outer route reaches a ford into the other side's outer jungle: the wraparound path for flank ganks and counter-jungling.

What the data model needs

Today's World.json authors walls as variable-width splines, which the generator raises as ridges. That model produced the strips you rejected, and it has no way to say "keep this clearing at full size". This plan needs World.json to author the open spaces instead: clearings, routes with width classes, and keep perimeters with gates. The generator would then derive the rock and forest masses from what's left, under the thickness and area rules above. Runtime collision and sight would read those derived masses, either as polygons or broken into the boxes ADR-043 already uses. This changes ADR-043 §1 and ADR-040 §3, so it needs a new record, ADR-073, before implementation.

Decisions for you

D1

Map size

Half extent 10,000, +26% playable area, so the outer jungle can be 2,300 deep with a camp, loops and lane mouths. The alternative keeps 9,000 and pulls the side lanes in 900, which shrinks both the outer and inner jungle.

D2

Keep entrances

Three lane gates only. The alternative adds one narrow side door per keep into each inner jungle, which gives defenders a flank but weakens the wall's purpose.

D3

Mid lane length

Mid walks in 47 s instead of 39 s, because the keeps sit deeper in the corners. Side lanes barely change. Smaller keeps (walls at 5,000) would bring mid to about 44 s.

D4

Flux Well lean

Each team reaches its bot-side Well 3.5 s sooner than its top-side one (Team A: south 41.3 s, north 44.8 s; Team B mirrored). Keep it as a deliberate lean toward cross-map trades, or even it out.

D5

Jungle density

Interior masses at 25% of the jungle. Raise it toward 30 to 35% for more cover and sight-blocking, or lower it for more open fighting room.

D6

New camps' wildlife

The two new camps per team need species. Reuse two of the six existing ones (camps keep fixed identities, so a second Ashfang den is allowed), or add new Veyran wildlife to the Battleground Bible.

D7

Data model

Write ADR-073: author the playable spaces, derive the masses. This comes before any generation pass.

After you approve

  1. ADR-073 and the World.json schema change, with validation for route widths, mass thickness, fog clearance and half-turn fairness.
  2. Generator: derive the masses, keep walls and gates, river and Well arenas from the new data, then regenerate the map.
  3. Carry over the reusable parts of Pass14: the validator's probe fix, the rule keeping map fog off lanes with its test, and the 30-route navigation suite.
  4. Validate navigation for both teams, sight and fairness; capture gameplay-camera views and an honest whole-map overhead for your review.
  5. Retune Spire distances and wave timing for the new lane lengths, as data only.
