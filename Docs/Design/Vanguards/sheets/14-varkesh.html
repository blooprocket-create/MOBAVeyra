Lore

Varkesh is not a transformed human, a construct, or a Fluxborn.

Forgehearts form through centuries of repeated molten alloy, heat, pressure, smelting, and forging until elemental consciousness emerges.

Varkesh is old and respected and has worked with Iron March engineers for decades.

A roughly 3m figure — close to twice a person's height — built from dark interlocking iron plates over a molten interior, with a bright orange-white core spiral set into the chest. The seams between plates glow, and movement sheds sparks and molten fragments: plates at his extremities ride slightly clear of the body, and broken pieces hang in the air around a raised hand. A heavy cloth drape hangs at the waist, held by iron rings. His head is a faceless wedge of plate set low between enormous shoulders, crested with short spines and lit from inside like the rest of him. Both hands stay open and empty, fingers spread. Molten material tears loose from his plating and orbits them, ready to be thrown; a strip of ground has already gone molten where he is working, and at distance the leading edge of it is cooling into black iron. That is his whole offence and his silhouette has to say so — everything he throws is drawn off his own body. Behind him the Ember Basin: lava running in channels through a burning ruined city. The metal is his body, not armour worn over something else: never draw a person, pilot, or face inside the shell. He carries no weapon — never a sword, never an axe, never anything with a hilt or a forged edge. He is a Forgeheart — an elemental consciousness that formed in the Ember Basin crucibles — not a golem, a construct, a Fluxborn, or a transformed human.

Ability kit
Passive

Stress Temper

Damaging abilities coat enemy Vanguards in Heated Metal.

If a Heated enemy dashes or blinks, the coating rapidly cools at the endpoint:

- magic damage; - brief root.

Q

Slagshot

Launch molten material to a target area.

Small AoE magic damage + Heated Metal application.

W

Tempered Shell

Cool Varkesh's outer body into black metal.

Gain a substantial shield and resistance to slows/control while the shield holds.

When the shield breaks/expires, heat bursts outward for minor damage.

E

Molten Ground

Create a broad medium-range strip of molten terrain.

Enemies are slowed and entering the area applies Heated Metal.

Dashing across it creates a natural Stress Temper punishment.

R

Forge Divide

Launch a huge rolling wall/wave of molten metal.

Deals heavy magic damage, slightly pushes enemies away from the centerline, and applies Heated Metal.

At maximum distance, the wave cools into a temporary solid black-iron wall that blocks both teams.
