# Veyra Custom Matches & Private Lobbies Bible

**Version:** 0.1 — Invite-only sandbox match decisions checkpoint  
**Status:** Working design canon for rules explicitly marked *Locked*; detailed host controls, lifecycle and edge cases still need design  
**Scope:** Custom lobby entry and host permissions, human/AI team composition, selection access, bot difficulty, adjustable match rules, invited spectator-only guests and delay configuration, rewards and replay cross-references.  
**Related:** [Modes & Access](Veyra_Modes_Access_Bible_v0.1.md) owns matchmade mode/rotation policy; [Client & Platform](Veyra_Client_Platform_Bible_v0.1.md) owns pre-game and Unreal client handoffs; [Parties & Social](Veyra_Parties_Social_Matchmaking_Bible_v0.1.md) owns friends/blocks and matchmade queues; [Account, Collection & Mastery](Veyra_Account_Collection_Mastery_Bible_v0.1.md) owns reward exclusions and Vanguard entitlement; [Replay & Spectating](Veyra_Replay_Spectator_Bible_v0.1.md) owns playback and retention; [Match Flow](Veyra_Match_Flow_Bible_v0.1.md) owns standard-match adjudication.

> **Design intent:** A custom match is a host-configured sandbox for experimentation and practice, **not** a matchmade PvP/Co-op queue, a way to grant account rewards, or a bypass of human Vanguard ownership. The trusted game server still validates the lobby configuration, participant identity, selections and gameplay. Exact host UI, preset data and infrastructure are not yet specified.

## 1. Locked — invite-only custom lobbies

- A custom lobby is **invite-only**: no public custom-lobby browser, no publicly discoverable rooms and no matchmaking of strangers into open seats. The host invites the human participants they want.
- The host decides which human player occupies which team/slot. Custom-lobby membership and host assignment are distinct from matchmaking-party ownership/readiness; being in an ordinary five-player party is not required to design a custom match.
- The host may fill vacant positions with AI on **either team**, including friendly bots, opposing bots and mixed human/AI teams. This permission is **custom-only**: matchmade Co-op vs AI still requires five human allies versus five opposing AI and **never** substitutes friendly bots.
- The ordinary battleground has two teams with up to five Vanguard slots each. **Custom matches do not require all ten slots occupied**, symmetrical sides, equal humans or an opponent. A host can launch **1v1, 1v5, mixed human/bot, or solo 1v0 practice**, so long as at least one valid human player is in the match. Empty slots stay empty unless the host explicitly places a bot; no automatic friendly-bot fill.
- Solo practice means **one human Vanguard on the battleground with no other player or AI Vanguard**, not a separately required matchmaking queue. **Solo 1v0 launches in open-ended practice mode** rather than awarding an instant victory for the missing opposition. The host ends practice when they choose (see §4).

## 2. Locked — human selection vs AI Vanguard choice

- **Every human must permanently own the Vanguard they select or have that Vanguard available through the current weekly free rotation.** This applies independently to the host and each invited player; host privileges and custom lobby options cannot confer human entitlement.
- **Any AI slot may use any released Vanguard**, regardless of what the host or the other humans own and regardless of weekly rotation eligibility. The host chooses each bot's Vanguard independently.
- **The host may configure Vanguard-selection rules** for custom matches, but those rules cannot permit a human to select an unowned Vanguard outside their free rotation. Detailed restrictions or allowances for duplicates, cross-team mirrors, bans, draft order and AI-vs-human duplicates **remain to be designed** rather than silently copying the default matchmade rules.
- Selection eligibility, the current rotation snapshot, AI release availability and any trade/reassignment that changes a human's assigned Vanguard must be validated by trusted services, not just a client dropdown.

## 3. Locked — per-bot AI difficulty

- The host sets **each bot's difficulty separately**. Current defined AI behaviors include **Beginner** and **Intermediate**; different difficulties may be mixed within the same team or between opposing teams.
- A custom lobby could contain Beginner bots alongside one human against Intermediate bots, but the normal matchmade Beginner/Intermediate Co-op queues remain separate and retain their fixed all-enemy-AI composition.
- Additional AI behavior tiers and whether they are available in customs require separate design; merely having a per-bot selector does not create an unapproved new tier.

## 4. Locked — host-adjustable sandbox match rules

- The host may **change gameplay settings for the custom lobby**, including starting in-match **Gold**, **objective timers**, **Vanguard selection rules**, and **whether normal battleground victory conditions are enabled**. Players can experiment with nonstandard setups without altering standard Casual, Draft, Co-op or eventual Ranked.
- The host may decide how many permitted human/AI Vanguard slots are occupied and which team each occupies. An intentionally uneven team composition is valid.
- **Open-ended practice is a supported custom match configuration:** the host can disable normal victory conditions so destroying the enemy base or having no opposing team does **not** automatically declare a winner or end the session. The match continues until the host chooses **End Custom Match**. This applies to solo 1v0 practice by default and may also be used for populated custom games. The host may instead leave normal victory enabled for custom games with an opposing side. **End Custom Match** is a host-authorized command validated and resolved by the match authority, not a client-decided victory or surrender vote; it produces a completed practice/custom session with an explicit **host-ended/no competitive winner** result rather than awarding a fabricated win or loss. Exact host handoff on disconnection, emergency idle/session timeout and midmatch settings changes remain open.
- Custom rule values must be represented as **editable validated game/mode data** and applied **only to that custom session** through an authoritative session configuration. Do not hardcode magic numbers, trust arbitrary client-supplied rules, overwrite global battleground defaults or reuse custom overrides in matchmade queues.
- This is permission to build a host-adjustable sandbox, **not** advance approval of every hypothetical option. Exact supported settings, safe ranges, which rules are immutable for technical integrity, match start procedure, interactions with optional victory conditions, pause/vote handling and available presets remain separate design work.
- Custom games still use the normal server-authoritative combat/match rules unless a specific host-adjustable override has been explicitly defined; custom mode does not grant the host live-game authority over damage, results, purchases or player accounts.

## 5. Locked — progression and replay compatibility

- Custom/private matches award **no persistent Account XP and no Vanguard Mastery**, regardless of whether the host uses normal settings, one player, bots, or a full ten-human match. Human-owned Vanguards remain owned; a custom bot's selected Vanguard grants no human ownership or mastery.
- Other possible custom rewards/achievements, if any, are **not approved**; do not infer rewards from duration, win/loss, bot difficulty or custom setup.
- **Every custom match produces a gameplay replay recording**, including solo or uneven practice matches, under the [Replay & Spectating Bible](Veyra_Replay_Spectator_Bible_v0.1.md). Its agreed link/share, server-side Save Replay slots, retention and moderator-only chat permissions remain intact unless changed deliberately in replay design.
- An **invite-only live custom lobby** does not, by itself, imply that completed replay links are participant-only: existing replay canon permits anyone with an available link to view/save. **Invited spectator-only guests may watch live customs as described in §6**, without becoming match participants or overriding the established public-replay rules.
- Match participation is needed to submit a match-specific report; being an invited guest, bot owner or replay viewer does not grant participant-report rights for matches one did not play.

## 6. Locked — invited spectator-only guests and host-configurable live delay

- The host may invite **spectator-only guests** into the private custom lobby. Spectator guests **do not occupy either team's Vanguard slots**, do not select/control a Vanguard and cannot change the match's gameplay or outcomes. They watch via Unreal **Spectator Mode** using the agreed replay-stream/camera infrastructure; a custom 1v1, uneven game, or solo practice session may have spectators independently of its playing roster.
- **The custom-lobby host may choose whether spectator-only guests watch with the usual three-minute delay or with the delay disabled (live / zero delay)** for coaching, practice or community events. The selection applies to that custom match's invited spectator-only guests and is a trusted, validated **pre-match lobby configuration**, not a client-controlled playback bypass. Display the configured delay state to custom-lobby participants before launch so no one unexpectedly plays an undelayed observed match.
- **Ordinary friend spectating of matchmade games retains the three-minute delay.** Joining a normal friend's in-progress match is **not** permission to obtain a zero-delay feed. For custom matches, only a host-authorized **invited spectator-only seat** receives the custom lobby's selected delay mode; a generic friend-spectate entry point or completed public replay link does not grant custom spectator authorization or bypass its rules.
- Whether delayed or undelayed, spectator-only guests have the established free camera/participant perspectives within their permitted playback frontier; no spectator-to-player communication or gameplay influence is provided **inside Spectator Mode**. The host's zero-delay choice explicitly allows immediate external coaching or commentary; it does not claim to prevent friends communicating outside Veyra. Public spectator/replay UI still excludes archived/in-match/team/party chat evidence.
- An undelayed custom spectator must be shown only the custom match they were authorized to enter; configurable custom delay must **never** change standard matchmade spectating or leak another match's unreleased timeline. Exact permission checks, invitation lifetime and launch/reconnect timing remain implementation design.

## 7. Deliberately open — resolve before implementation

- Exact custom configuration menu: preset/custom modes, editable rule list, valid ranges, timers, item/gold/level starts, lanes/objective behavior, draft/duplicate/mirror policy, optional bot automation and permissions for host changes after all players Ready.
- **1v0 and other no-opponent sessions:** solo automatically uses open-ended practice with **no instant victory** and a host-controlled End Custom Match action. Define whether objectives/Fluxborn spawn, the exact end-result schema, behavior when no host remains, safe session lifetime limits, and detailed replay/completion metadata without turning an intentional manual end into a competitive win/loss.
- How to distinguish custom-lobby host from normal party leader, invite eligibility and expiration, ready/start consent, late join/rejoin, departure/host transfer, and team change while a session is launching.
- Invited spectator-only guest capacity, invite/acceptance flow, removal, midmatch admission, reconnect, possible host transfer, and detailed authorization/revocation. The host's **pre-match choice of three-minute or zero spectator delay is locked**, but whether that choice can change midmatch is not approved.
- Bot AI behaviors and navigation under modified objective timers, very uneven teams, absent enemy players and solo practice.
- Tests: one-human solo launch remains live until **host-authorized End Custom Match**, without immediate victory; a full opposing team can also practice with normal victory disabled; host ending emits an explicit noncompetitive completed-session result and finalized replay; 1v1 and uneven sides start with only assigned slots; an allied AI is allowed **only** in customs; AI can use an otherwise unowned released Vanguard while its human player cannot; per-bot mixed difficulties; spectator-only guests consume no Vanguard slots and cannot control gameplay; the host's announced three-minute versus zero-delay spectator choice is enforced server-side; friend spectating of matchmade games remains delayed; custom starting Gold/objective settings never affect matchmade games; custom match grants no Account XP/Mastery; replay is created for nonstandard compositions.

**This bible captures sandbox product requirements, including optional normal victory and a host-ended open-ended practice session. Backend service design, exact presets and outstanding lifecycle/end-condition edge cases are not yet implementation-approved.**
