How Veyra plays

Built around Flux

The Crucible gives you lanes, a jungle, objectives, Gold and experience. What your team does with them is yours to decide.

Flux is shared

Your whole team builds Team Flux together. It strengthens your lanes' pressure and unlocks tactical Flux Spells, so every player's work counts.

No roles forced

Five Vanguards load in and the team decides where they go. The map sets the stakes; it never assigns your part.

Readable first

Every ability reads clearly in the middle of a fight, however spectacular it looks. You always know what just hit you.
